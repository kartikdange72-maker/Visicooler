Conclusion

Q1. Was Cavin’s visible inside visi coolers?
Ans: Out of 127 verified outlets, 103 outlets were open. Among these, 99 outlets had visi coolers. Cavin’s was present in 62 out of 99 visi cooler outlets, while Cavin’s was not present in 37 outlets.

Q2. How was Cavin’s facing visibility?
Ans: Among the 62 Cavin’s-present outlets, 24 outlets had below 3 facings, 23 outlets had 3–5 facings, 13 outlets had 6–9 facings, and only 2 outlets had above 10 facings.

Q3. How was Cavin’s rack placement?
Ans: Cavin’s rack placement has been counted rack-wise. If Cavin’s was present on multiple racks such as Rack 1,2, the count has been added to both Rack 1 and Rack 2 instead of creating a separate Rack 1,2 bucket. After this correction, Cavin’s placement is highest on Rack 1 with 24 counts, followed by Rack 2 with 20 counts, Rack 3 with 14 counts, Rack 4 with 10 counts, and Rack 5 with 1 count.

Q4. What is the overall conclusion?
Ans: Cavin’s was present in 62 cooler outlets, but visibility strength was mixed. A large share of outlets had lower facing depth, with 24 outlets below 3 facings. Rack visibility is now counted across actual shelves, showing strongest presence on Rack 1 and Rack 2, while deeper rack visibility remains weaker.
